# DPIA summary and vendor information - RetailCredit Score v3

*Synthetic document for the SAAF hackathon.*

## DPIA (summary)
A DPIA was completed on 10 January 2026. Legal ground for processing: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The DPIA states that automated declines are necessary for entering into the loan agreement. Customers who are declined receive a standard letter stating that "your application does not meet our acceptance criteria". The letter does not explain the main factors behind the decision and does not mention the right to obtain human intervention.

## Fundamental rights
No fundamental rights impact assessment has been performed. The project team considered the DPIA sufficient.

## Vendor (ScoreCo B.V.)
ScoreCo B.V. is registered in the bank's register of information for ICT third-party service providers as a provider supporting an important function. The contract includes audit and access rights for Polderbank and its supervisors. An exit strategy has not yet been documented.

## Instructions for use
ScoreCo provided a two-page user guide describing the score range and reason codes. The guide does not state the accuracy for sub-groups or the conditions under which the model should not be used.
